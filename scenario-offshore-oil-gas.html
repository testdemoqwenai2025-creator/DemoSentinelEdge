Platform Profile

Atlantis-7 — Mississippi Canyon Block 713

A deepwater offshore production platform operated by DeepWater Energy Partners. Installed in 2018-06-15 with a 30-year design life. The platform processes oil and gas from 4 subsea wells, separates and compresses gas for export, and stores crude before pipeline transport to shore.
