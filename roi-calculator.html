How the calculator works

The math, transparently.

Every number above comes from our published benchmarks. Here's exactly what each formula is:

Downtime savings

annual_downtime_cost × downtime_reduction_pct

For manufacturing (−42% benchmark): $4M × 0.42 = $1.68M annual savings. Validated against 3 design-partner pilots and Augury's published benchmarks.

Calibration savings

annual_calibration_cost × 0.85

Our self-calibration mesh (physics redundancy + sparse ground truth + GNN) cuts recalibration cost by 85% — validated against EPA AQS reference network over 12 months.

Yield uplift (agri only)

annual_revenue × 0.18

Sentinel-2/1 imagery + soil probe + weather station fusion delivers +18% yield across 3 design-partner farms in the 2025 season.

Platform cost

platform_base_month × 12 + sensor_count × sensor_cost_month × 12

Per-sensor subscription + base platform fee. Scales linearly with sensor estate. No setup fee for design partners.
