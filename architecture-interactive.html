How to use

Three ways to explore

1. Click a component

Click any box in the diagram to see its details: what it does, tech stack, throughput, protocol, and how it connects to the rest of the pipeline.

2. Play data flow

Hit "Play data flow" to watch a packet animate through the full pipeline — from sensor reading to alert routing. Follow the cyan highlight as it moves through each stage.

3. Hover to highlight

Hover over any component to highlight its incoming and outgoing connections. See exactly what data flows in and out.
