Modality 01

Vibration (MEMS tri-axial)

The most widely deployed industrial sensor modality. Tri-axial MEMS accelerometers sample up to 25.6 kHz, compute features (RMS, kurtosis, FFT peaks) on-device, and stream 1 Hz feature vectors over MQTT or OPC-UA. Used for rotating machinery health, bearing fault detection, structural integrity monitoring.

SpecValue
Sample rateup to 25.6 kHz (raw), 1 Hz (feature stream)
Range±16g / ±50g / ±200g (depends on sensor)
Resolution16-24 bit
Axes3 (X, Y, Z) simultaneously
Edge deviceHailo-8 (typical) / Jetson Orin Nano (heavy)
Calibration interval180 days
Use casesBearing faults, gear faults, imbalance, misalignment, structural integrity

Modality 02

Gas / chemical (multi-pixel)

Multi-pixel gas sensors (Sensirion, ScioSense) provide a chemical fingerprint rather than a single ppm reading. Used for industrial emissions, air quality, leak detection, process chemistry fingerprinting.

SpecValue
Sample rate1 Hz typical
Pixels4-8 (depending on model)
Detection range0-1000 ppm (typical), varies by gas
Edge deviceRPi 5 + Coral (low-power)
Calibration interval90 days
WANLoRaWAN / NB-IoT typical
Use casesIndustrial emissions, air quality, leak detection, process chemistry, food freshness
Modality 03

Vision / hyperspectral (RGB + thermal + 31-band)

Vision runs the gamut from $50 RGB cameras for defect detection to $20k hyperspectral imagers for material identification. The platform ingests frames as gRPC streams and runs CV models at the edge — typically YOLO-class for object detection, ResNet-class for classification, and custom transformers for hyperspectral.

SpecRGB + thermalHyperspectral
Frame rate30 fps10 fps
Resolution1920×1080 typical640×480 × 31 bands
Edge deviceJetson Orin NX (heavy)Jetson Orin NX + custom FPGA
CalibrationIntrinsics (camera optics)180 days (spectral)
Use casesDefect detection, safety, OEEMaterial ID, food quality, crop stress, pharmaceutical QC
Modality 04

mmWave radar (60 GHz)

Non-contact micro-motion detection through walls and obstructions. Used for vital signs monitoring, occupancy, gesture recognition, and micro-motion in industrial settings (vibration in places where accelerometers can't reach).

SpecValue
Frequency60 GHz (typical) — also 77 GHz automotive
Sample rate20 Hz (range-Doppler frames)
Range0.2 - 5 m typical (depends on antenna)
Edge deviceCoral Edge TPU (low-power)
Calibration365 days (very stable)
Use casesVital signs, occupancy, gesture, micro-motion through walls
Modality 05

Soil probes (NPK + pH + EC)

Precision agriculture's primary ground truth. Multi-parameter probes measure nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), potassium (K), pH, and electrical conductivity at multiple depths. Sampled every 15 minutes (battery conservation), transported over LoRaWAN.

SpecValue
Sample rateEvery 15 min
ParametersN, P, K, pH, EC, soil moisture, temperature
Probe depths15, 30, 60 cm typical (multi-depth)
Edge deviceRPi 5 + LoRaWAN gateway
Calibration30 days (high drift)
Use casesPrecision agriculture, irrigation optimization, contamination tracking
Soil probes drift fast

Soil probes are one of the highest-drift sensors we handle — 30-day calibration interval is typical. This is where the self-calibration mesh pays off most: using neighboring probes + physics (water flow models) to keep drift in check.

Modality 06

Weather stations (12 parameters)

Microclimate grids for severe weather nowcasting, renewable generation forecasting, and agricultural decision support. The platform ingests from any weather station that speaks Modbus or LoRaWAN.

SpecValue
Sample rate1 minute typical
ParametersTemperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed/direction, precipitation, solar radiation, UV, dew point, etc.
Edge deviceRPi 5 + LoRaWAN gateway (typical)
Calibration365 days (very stable)
Use casesMicroclimate, severe weather nowcasting, solar forecasting, irrigation scheduling
Modality 07

AIS maritime (vessel tracking)

Automatic Identification System — VHF-broadcast vessel position reports every 2 seconds for equipped vessels. Combined with satellite AIS for ocean coverage. Used for vessel tracking, illegal fishing detection, supply chain disruption signals.

SpecValue
Sample rate2 s (VHF), 1 min (satellite)
FieldsMMSI, lat/lon, SOG, COG, heading, status, vessel type, destination
Edge deviceCloud (no edge needed)
Calibrationn/a (self-reported by vessels)
Use casesFishing legality, supply chain disruption, port congestion, maritime safety

EO is the only modality where the "sensor" is a satellite. We fuse EO with ground sensors — Sentinel-2 crop stress signals anchored by soil probe ground truth, Sentinel-5P air quality calibrated by ground-level PM2.5 sensors. This fusion is what makes our agriculture and environment verticals work.
