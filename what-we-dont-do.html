What We Don't Do (Yet)

Counter-intuitive page, but investors trust honesty more than marketing. Here's an honest list of what AISensorEdgeComp doesn't do today, why, and when we plan to close each gap. No spin.

Why this page exists

Most investor pages list what the company does. We list both. Investors who do technical due diligence will find the gaps anyway — better to surface them ourselves, with the context of why the gap exists and when we plan to close it. Honesty builds trust; trust closes deals.

Each gap below has: (1) what we don't do, (2) why we don't do it yet, (3) when we plan to close the gap, (4) what the workaround is in the meantime.

What we deliberately won't do

Some gaps are choices, not roadmap items. Here's what we've consciously decided not to build:

Sell raw data

We never sell raw sensor data. Federated learning shares gradients only (with DP noise + SGX). Selling raw data would break customer trust and the federated learning moat.

Cloud lock-in

The platform runs on any k8s — AWS, Azure, GCP, on-prem. We don't have a "preferred cloud" partnership. Customer choice > cloud referral fees.

Compete with sensor OEMs

We don't manufacture sensors. We ingest from any. Partnering with SKF, Endress+Hauser, Emerson — not competing — is the strategy.

Black-box AI

Every alert has a citation chain. Every causal claim has a do-calculus test result. We will never ship AI that can't explain itself.
