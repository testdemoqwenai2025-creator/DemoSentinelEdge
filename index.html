01 / The Open Frontier

The world is instrumented.
The data is not intelligent.

50 billion sensors will be deployed by 2030. They generate exabytes per day. Yet the intelligence layer that should turn this torrent into decisions is fragmented, brittle, and locked inside vertical silos. The opportunity is not another sensor — it is the universal substrate that makes every sensor queryable, calibratable, and reason-able.

01 Protocol Heterogeneity

A single factory floor commonly runs Modbus, OPC-UA, Profinet, MQTT, CoAP, and proprietary serial — all at once. Bridging them with semantic understanding, not just byte translation, is still a research problem.

02 Calibration Drift

Every sensor degrades. A 10,000-node deployment costs $1M+/year in truck rolls to recalibrate. No platform has solved self-calibrating networks at scale.

03 Edge-Cloud Workload Placement

Most platforms hardcode "ML runs in cloud, rules run at edge." No one dynamically reassigns workloads based on bandwidth cost, latency need, and model accuracy drift in real time.

04 Unlabeled Industrial Data

Most industrial IoT data has no labels. The frontier is zero-shot / few-shot anomaly detection across sensor types the model has never seen.

04 / The Intelligence

Foundation models meet physical reality.

The 2024 emergence of time-series foundation models changed what was possible. We extend them with sensor-fusion transformers, edge-cloud co-inference, and causal reasoning — turning pattern recognition into decisions.

Time-Series Foundation Model core

Pre-trained on public industrial corpora (NASA bearings, Case Western Reserve, SECOM, ARPA-E) plus scraped OPC-UA streams. Deploy to a new factory, zero labeled data, surface anomalies immediately.

pretrain → few-shot adapt → zero-shot detect

Edge-Cloud Liquid Placement infra

Scheduler watches bandwidth cost, latency need, model accuracy drift, and battery state across a fleet of edge devices. Dynamically reassigns inference jobs. No more hardcoded "edge vs cloud."

observe → score → realloc → verify

Federated & Privacy-Preserving trust
