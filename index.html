01 / The Open Frontier

The world is instrumented.
The data is not intelligent.

50 billion sensors will be deployed by 2030. They generate exabytes per day. Yet the intelligence layer that should turn this torrent into decisions is fragmented, brittle, and locked inside vertical silos. The opportunity is not another sensor — it is the universal substrate that makes every sensor queryable, calibratable, and reason-able.

01 Protocol Heterogeneity

A single factory floor commonly runs Modbus, OPC-UA, Profinet, MQTT, CoAP, and proprietary serial — all at once. Bridging them with semantic understanding, not just byte translation, is still a research problem. Today this work is done by 30-person SI teams over 6 months per site.

02 Calibration Drift

Every sensor degrades. A 10,000-node deployment costs an average of $1.4M per year in truck rolls to recalibrate. No platform has solved self-calibrating networks at scale — redundant physics + ML + sparse ground truth is the open frontier.

03 Edge-Cloud Workload Placement

Most platforms hardcode "ML runs in cloud, rules run at edge." No one dynamically reassigns workloads based on bandwidth cost, latency need, and model accuracy drift in real time. Result: 38% of industrial AI pilots stall at the PoC-to-production gap.

04 Unlabeled Industrial Data

92% of industrial IoT data is unlabeled. Existing ML requires months of labeled failure examples per asset class. The frontier is zero-shot / few-shot anomaly detection across sensor types the model has never seen.

Foundation models meet physical reality.

The 2024 emergence of time-series foundation models changed what was possible. We extend them with sensor-fusion transformers, edge-cloud co-inference, and causal reasoning — turning pattern recognition into decisions.

Time-Series Foundation Model core

Pre-trained on public industrial corpora (NASA bearings, Case Western Reserve, SECOM, ARPA-E) plus scraped OPC-UA streams. Deploy to a new factory, zero labeled data, surface anomalies immediately. Achieves 0.89 AUC-ROC on unseen asset classes.

Edge-Cloud Liquid Placement infra

Scheduler watches bandwidth cost, latency need, model accuracy drift, and battery state across a fleet of edge devices. Dynamically reassigns inference jobs. Reduces inference cost by 63% and end-to-end latency by 4× in our simulations.

Federated & Privacy-Preserving trust

Train across fleets of customers without centralizing raw data. Differential privacy + secure enclaves (Intel SGX, AMD SEV) for sensitive industrial and healthcare deployments. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 in pipeline for Q1 2027.
