Design Partner Onboarding

The 60-day design partner pilot

Our design partner program is a 60-day, free evaluation with full platform access and engineering support. Here's what happens at each stage:

Week 0 — Sign up

  • Sign up via the self-service portal
  • We review your application within 24 hours
  • Sandbox tenant provisioned with your synthetic data

Week 1 — Onboarding

  • Kickoff call with founding team (30 min)
  • We onboard your real sensor estate (up to 50k tags) — 2-week SLA
  • Edge cluster shipped to your site (Hailo-8 + Jetson Orin)
  • Grafana dashboard configured for your vertical

Weeks 2-4 — Baseline

  • Platform ingests your sensor data in parallel with existing systems
  • TS-FM runs zero-shot anomaly detection on your streams
  • Weekly engineering check-in (30 min)
  • First anomaly report delivered at end of week 4

Weeks 5-8 — Evaluation

  • Compare platform alerts vs. your existing systems
  • LLM-native query layer available for your operators
  • ROI assessment using our ROI calculator
  • Final readout: convert to 3-year contract or walk away

What's included

  • Full platform access (no feature gating)
  • Up to 50k sensor tags ingested and normalized
  • 1 edge cluster on your site
  • Custom Grafana dashboard
  • Weekly engineering check-ins
  • Anomaly detection report at end of pilot

What's not included

  • No SLA (best-effort during pilot)
  • No production support (design partner only)
  • No data export to your ERP during pilot (read-only)
