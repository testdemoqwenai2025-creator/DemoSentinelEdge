Culture

What we value

Provenance over performance

Every AI output carries a citation chain. Every commit carries a review chain. Every decision carries a rationale. We are building infrastructure for industrial buyers who need audit trails; we live it ourselves.

Edge autonomy

The platform must run for 72 hours without WAN. So must the team. We don't have on-call rotation — we have engineers who own their systems end-to-end. We trust them to be on or off.

Vertical depth

Horizontal platforms are a dime a dozen. We win by going deep in verticals. Every engineer spends 1 week per quarter shadowing a design-partner customer. Every salesperson can demo the TS-FM.

Customer data is sacred

Per-customer encryption keys. Federated learning only. No employee has access to raw sensor data — only to encrypted blobs + derived metadata. This is the SOC 2 commitment; it's also our culture.
