Go-to-market motion

Design partners → paid pilots → expansion

Each vertical goes through the same motion: 60-day paid pilot with 1-3 design partners → convert to multi-year contract → expand within account → use as reference for next 5 design partners in same vertical.

Phase 1: Design partner

60-day paid pilot, $5k-25k. Free engineering support. We commit to onboarding your sensor estate (up to 50k tags) within 2 weeks. You commit to weekly engineering check-in.

Phase 2: Convert to contract

3-year contract, $50k-500k/year depending on sensor estate. Includes platform access, model updates, 24/7 support SLA.

Phase 3: Expand + reference

Expand within account (more sites, more verticals). Use as reference for next 5 design partners in same vertical. Network effect compounds.
