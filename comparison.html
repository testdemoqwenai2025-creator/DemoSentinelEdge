How we differ

AISensorEdgeComp

We are the only platform with a TS-FM foundation model + federated learning + causal inference + liquid edge placement + carbon-aware routing. The universal substrate — not plumbing, not a single vertical.

AWS IoT Core
Cloud IoT infrastructure
They sell plumbing. No intelligence layer, no foundation model, no edge ML autonomy, no federated learning, no carbon-aware routing. We integrate with them (customers can use S3/IoT Core as ingest) but the reasoning layer is ours.
☁️
Azure IoT Hub

Same story as AWS. Cloud infrastructure, no intelligence. Azure ML is a separate product you bolt on. No foundation model, no edge autonomy, no federated learning.
🏭
Cognite
Industrial data ops
They go deep in industrial data modeling. Great data ops, but no ML foundation model, no edge compute, no federated learning. They're a data layer; we're an intelligence layer. We complement, not compete — our CEO came from Cognite.
🌀
Augury
Vibration anomaly detection
They go deep in one modality (vibration). Excellent models, but single-sensor-type only. We're the universal substrate — every modality, every vertical. Our Chief Scientist came from Augury and built their TS-FM.
📊
Uptake
Predictive analytics
Bespoke predictive models per customer. No foundation model, no zero-shot. Each new asset class requires months of labeled data. Our TS-FM works zero-shot on any asset class.
🔍
Seeq
Process manufacturing analytics
Great analytics tool for process engineers. But it's a desktop/server tool, not a platform. No ingest, no edge, no streaming, no ML, no federated learning. Seeq is what you use after AISensorEdgeComp has ingested and processed your data.
🔧
ThingsBoard
Open-source IoT platform
Great open-source plumbing. Self-hosted, free, but no ML, no foundation model, no edge AI, no federated learning. DIY path — you build the intelligence layer yourself. We save you 18 months.
Where we're weaker

Honest gaps — we don't hide them.

Scale of existing deployments

AWS IoT and Azure IoT have billions of devices connected. We have 7 design partners. That's the pre-Series A reality — and the reason we're raising. See our what-we-dont-do page for the full gap list.

SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / IEC 62443

AWS, Azure, Cognite, Augury, Uptake all have SOC 2 Type II. We're in flight (Q1 2027). For now, design partners use our air-gapped deployment option for sensitive workloads.

Ecosystem breadth

AWS IoT has 200+ integrations in their marketplace. We have 12 ingest protocols + our SDK (Python, JS). Our roadmap closes this with Go (Q4 2026), Rust (Q1 2027), and 50+ connectors by end of 2027.

Brand recognition

Cognite is a $4B+ unicorn. Augury is a $1B+ company. We're pre-Series A. That changes with the round close in Q4 2026 — but today, an AWS IoT stamp on a slide deck opens doors we can't.

Methodology

How we built this comparison

Each cell is based on: (1) public documentation from the competitor, (2) our design-partner evaluations (customers who used competitor X before us), (3) Gartner / Forrester reports where available, (4) our own internal testing. Where we're unsure, we mark "◐" (partial). Where we're clearly weaker, we say so. This page is reviewed quarterly — last update: September 2026.

If you're from one of the companies listed and believe we've mischaracterized you, email platform@aisensoredgecomp.ai — we'll correct it within 48 hours. We want this to be the most accurate comparison in the industry, not a marketing hit piece.
